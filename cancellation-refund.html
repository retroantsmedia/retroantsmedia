Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 5 November 2026

At RETRO ANTS MEDIA, we value our customers and strive to provide professional services. This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains the terms applicable when customers cancel bookings, reschedule assignments, request refunds or discontinue orders.

By booking our services or placing an order, customers agree to the following policy.

01

Booking Cancellation by Customers

  1. Customers who wish to cancel a confirmed booking must inform RETRO ANTS MEDIA through email, phone, WhatsApp or other written communication.
  2. If a customer cancels a booking due to personal reasons, unavoidable circumstances or other issues, RETRO ANTS MEDIA will discuss the situation with the customer and review whether a refund is applicable.
  3. If the cancellation occurs before any service or work has started, the customer will generally be eligible for a full refund of the amount paid.
  4. If the service or work has already started, the refund will be considered based on the work completed and expenses already incurred.
  5. Cancellation requests made close to the event date may involve additional charges depending on the arrangements and expenses already incurred.
02

Rescheduling of Bookings

  1. Customers who wish to change their event date or booking schedule must inform RETRO ANTS MEDIA as early as possible.
  2. Rescheduling requests are subject to the availability of our photography and videography team.
  3. If the requested date is available, the booking may be transferred to the new date based on mutual agreement.
  4. If additional travel, accommodation, equipment or other arrangements are required, additional charges may apply.
  5. If the requested date is unavailable, both parties may discuss alternative dates or cancellation arrangements.
03

Cancellation by RETRO ANTS MEDIA

  1. In exceptional circumstances where RETRO ANTS MEDIA is unable to fulfil a confirmed assignment, we will inform the customer as early as possible.
  2. We will make reasonable efforts to arrange an alternative team or mutually acceptable rescheduled date.
  3. If the assignment cannot be completed and no alternative arrangement is accepted, the customer will receive a full refund of the amount paid for the services that were not provided.
  4. The refund details will be communicated to the customer, and the approved refund will be processed within 3 working days of confirmation.
  5. Refunds will generally be made through the original payment method wherever possible.
04

Photo and Video Editing Services

  1. If a customer requests cancellation before editing work has started, a full refund of the amount paid will generally be provided.
  2. Once editing work has started, cancellation requests will be evaluated based on the work completed.
  3. Charges for completed editing work and expenses already incurred may be deducted from the refundable amount.
  4. If the editing work has been completed and delivered according to the agreed requirements, payment for that completed service will generally not be refundable.
  5. Additional revisions or changes outside the agreed scope may involve additional charges.
05

Album Designing, Printing and Customised Frames

  1. Once album printing, frame production or customised product manufacturing has started, cancellation may not be possible without applicable charges.
  2. Costs already incurred for designing, printing, materials, production and customisation may be deducted from any eligible refund.
  3. If a product is defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied due to an error on our part, customers should contact us with the relevant details and supporting photographs.
  4. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate solution, such as correction, replacement or refund, as applicable.
06

How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Customers can submit cancellation, rescheduling or refund requests through the following communication channels:

  • Email.
  • Phone call.
  • WhatsApp.
  • Written communication.

Please include the following details when submitting a request:

  • Customer name.
  • Booking or order reference number, if available.
  • Date of booking or order.
  • Service or product details.
  • Reason for cancellation or refund.
  • Payment transaction reference, where necessary.

Our team will review the request and communicate the applicable terms and next steps.

07

Changes to This Policy

RETRO ANTS MEDIA reserves the right to update or modify this Cancellation & Refund Policy when necessary.

Any changes will be published on this website along with the revised date. Customers are encouraged to review this page periodically.

08

Contact Information

For questions or clarification regarding cancellations, rescheduling or refunds, please contact us through our official communication channels.
