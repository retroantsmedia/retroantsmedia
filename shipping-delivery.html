RETRO ANTS MEDIA

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: 5 November 2026

At RETRO ANTS MEDIA, we aim to deliver all completed photographs, videos, albums, customised frames and other products in a timely and professional manner. This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains our delivery methods, processing timelines, shipping arrangements and related conditions.

01

Delivery Methods

RETRO ANTS MEDIA provides digital and physical delivery options depending on the services and products ordered.

Digital Delivery:

  • Final photographs and edited images.
  • Edited videos and reels.
  • Photo and video editing projects, where agreed.
  • Digital album designs.
  • Other completed digital deliverables.

Digital files may be delivered through:

  • Google Drive or other cloud storage links.
  • Email.
  • WhatsApp, where suitable.
  • Downloadable links.

Physical Delivery:

  • Printed photo albums.
  • Customised photo frames.
  • Printed photographs.
  • Pen drives containing photographs and videos.
  • Other customised printing products.

Physical products may be delivered through courier services, postal services, local delivery or direct collection, depending on the customer's location and mutual agreement.

02

Digital Delivery Timelines

  1. Digital deliverables will be provided according to the timeline agreed upon during booking or order confirmation.
  2. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the project size, number of photographs, video duration, editing requirements and customer feedback.
  3. Album designs and other digital proofs will be shared for review where applicable.
  4. Customers must provide any required feedback, approvals or corrections in a timely manner to avoid delivery delays.
  5. Once the final deliverables are ready, customers will be notified through their registered contact details.
  6. Customers are responsible for downloading and maintaining backup copies of their delivered digital files.
03

Album Printing and Customised Product Delivery

  1. Album printing, customised frames and other physical products will be processed according to the specifications and requirements agreed upon with the customer.
  2. Production will begin after the necessary design approvals and payment requirements have been completed.
  3. Printing and manufacturing timelines depend on the product type, quantity, materials and production requirements.
  4. Customers will be informed when their products are ready for dispatch or collection.
  5. Any additional changes requested after production begins may involve additional charges and delivery time.
  6. We make reasonable efforts to ensure that all printed products meet the agreed quality and specifications.
04

Shipping and Delivery Charges

  1. Shipping and courier charges will be communicated to customers before order confirmation.
  2. Delivery charges may vary depending on the customer's location, product weight, dimensions and courier service.
  3. Local delivery, interstate shipping and other transportation arrangements may have different charges.
  4. Customers may also choose to collect their completed products directly, subject to prior arrangement.
  5. Any additional shipping charges arising from a customer-requested change of delivery address may be payable by the customer.
05

Delivery Address and Customer Information

  1. Customers must provide accurate and complete delivery information, including their name, phone number, address, city, state and PIN code.
  2. RETRO ANTS MEDIA will use the delivery information provided by the customer to arrange shipping and delivery.
  3. Customers must inform us of any address changes before the order is dispatched.
  4. RETRO ANTS MEDIA will not be responsible for delivery issues caused by incorrect or incomplete address details supplied by the customer.
06

Shipping and Courier Responsibility

  1. Physical products will be packed appropriately to minimise the risk of damage during transportation.
  2. Once an order has been dispatched, customers will be provided with available tracking information.
  3. Delivery timelines depend on the courier or postal service and the destination.
  4. RETRO ANTS MEDIA will make reasonable efforts to coordinate with the courier service in case of shipping delays or delivery issues.
  5. Customers are requested to inspect their packages upon receiving them and inform us promptly if any damage, missing items or incorrect products are identified.
07

Damaged, Missing or Incorrect Products

  1. If a customer receives a damaged, defective or incorrect product, they should contact RETRO ANTS MEDIA as soon as possible.
  2. Customers should provide photographs or videos showing the product condition and packaging as supporting evidence.
  3. We will review the complaint and coordinate an appropriate solution, which may include correction, replacement or refund, depending on the circumstances.
  4. If an error occurs during printing or production due to our mistake, we will take reasonable steps to correct the issue without additional charges to the customer.
  5. Issues caused by incorrect specifications or approvals provided by the customer will be reviewed separately.
08

Delivery Delays

Although we aim to deliver orders within the agreed timelines, delays may occur due to:

  • Courier or postal service delays.
  • Transportation disruptions.
  • Post Production or printing delays.
  • Unforeseen technical or operational issues.
  • Other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

We will communicate relevant updates to customers whenever possible.

11

Changes to This Policy

RETRO ANTS MEDIA reserves the right to update or modify this Shipping & Delivery Policy when necessary.

Any changes will be published on this website along with the revised date. Customers are encouraged to review this page periodically.

12

Contact Information

For questions or assistance regarding shipping, delivery, order tracking or physical products, please contact us.
